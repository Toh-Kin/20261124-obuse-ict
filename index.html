Policy: how Japan built this support

1
2018–2022 (extended to 2024)
5-year ICT plan sets the funded level: 1 facilitator per 4 schools
2
August 2021
Job title and duties defined in national regulations
3
2025–2027
3-year plan keeps 1 per 4 schools and adds a help desk
Funding: local government finance, part of each municipality's general revenue → each municipality decides.
The ICT budget (more than ¥140 billion a year, 2025–27) covers the whole ICT environment, not facilitators alone.
Sources: MEXT, 3-year plan for school ICT environment (2025–2027); MEXT notice on the School Education Act Enforcement Regulations (23 Aug 2021)

One form of this future: multi-track instruction

Single-track (one-way)
Teacher Gives the same input and instructions to everyone.
Learners Same activity, same pace, same place. Little choice.
Multi-track (interactive)
Teacher Short input, then sees each child’s progress and advises one by one.
Learners Choose their own pace and place. Look at each other’s work, from the blank page and in progress (peer referencing).
Source: Jun Takahashi, “Realizing multi-track instruction” (note, 24 Jul 2025). Illustrations drawn by the author.
